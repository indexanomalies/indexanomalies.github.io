Index Anomalies is a public digital vault dedicated to anonymous and pseudonymous masters.

For years, we have kept track of serious creators who refuse to play the branding game. The art world loves a face, a story, and a marketing pitch, but these makers chose another path. They built their work in silence and hid behind pseudonyms to keep things pure. We catalog what they left behind—raw physical objects and uncompromised records handed directly to the public without a corporate filter or an ego attached.

BLANK

PUBLIC ARCHIVE / CONCEPTUAL RECORD // STATUS: ARCHIVED

Named after the starting state of an empty sheet, Blank is an ongoing experiment where large pieces of unmarked paper are left sitting quietly in public hallways or busy streets. No instructions are given, no one watches over them, and nobody claims ownership.

Over time, regular people passing by mark them up—scribbled phone numbers, tired thoughts, sudden frustrations, and anonymous sketches.

The person behind the setup stays entirely out of it, leaving the project as a running log of what everyday crowds leave behind when handed a blank slate.

9FINGERS

PHYSICAL RECORD / STRUCTURAL ANOMALY // STATUS: ARCHIVED BASELINE

Some work chases whatever trend is hot this month, and then there is work that outlives the whole room because it was built heavy. Since its first canvases hit New York back in 1999 under a strict number pseudonym, 9Fingers has quietly stacked up a massive body of physical work that ignores commercial applause and gallery games.

The output comes from pure experience—knowing deep down that real work doesn't need a biography, a press agent, or an explanation. Built with thick layers of pigment and raw material that took decades to settle, each piece is a straight-up clash between heavy physical stuff and permanent presence. No copies, no compromises—just old-school weight that leaves typical contemporary stuff behind.

In this public registry, 9Fingers serves as the core pillar—a deeply established archive that runs entirely on its own clock.

LEAVES

WOOD & BOTANICAL / PUBLIC ARCHIVE // STATUS: ARCHIVED

For decades, the maker known as Leaves has put together objects that resist easy labels, staying completely clear of standard galleries.

The physical panels are built from discarded wood mixed with organic items—specifically dried leaves picked up quietly from city streets, forest floors, and abandoned lots. Rather than acting as decoration, these bits of nature give the panels a real sense of place and time.

Leaves has consistently turned down gallery representation and public openings, keeping the spotlight locked on the physical pieces alone.

NONAME

PHOTOGRAPHY / SPATIAL INTERVENTION // STATUS: ARCHIVED

Operating behind a clear pseudonym, Noname focuses on how public spaces and institutions work. Instead of trying to get into commercial galleries, photo interventions are placed directly out in the open streets without asking for permission first.

The core focus is simple: examining who gets to own public space and how money dictates who gets noticed.

GOLD

PAINTING / MONOCHROMIC ABSTRACTION // STATUS: ARCHIVED

Using a strict and uncompromising rule, Gold builds every single piece entirely in gold. By cutting out all other colors and contrasting palettes, the practice leans into sameness, creating a long line of panels where individual pieces tend to blur together.

Inside the index, Gold stands as a stubborn paradox—an endless loop on value, repetition, and how we spot differences inside a closed system.

HABORYM

PAINTING / HISTORICAL ARCHIVE // STATUS: ARCHIVED

Digging into old religious texts and folklore, Haborym focuses on pulling historical demonic figures out of old theological books and putting them back into plain sight. Instead of inventing new shapes, the work acts as an archive bringing old stories forward.

Every figure is set against a repeating red background that holds the series together while nodding to fire and old mythologies.
