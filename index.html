Index Anomalies is a public digital vault dedicated to anonymous and pseudonymous masters.

For years, we have maintained a public record of exceptional artists who choose to operate under pseudonyms. The world knows their creations, but their true identities remain protected. We catalog and present these undercover creators who have remained anonymous to this day—delivering uncompromised art directly to communities without a face or a brand attached.

OUR STANCE:

Art shouldn't need a public persona or marketing hype to matter. By keeping this domain completely sterile—free of ads, cookies, and tracking scripts—we provide a secure, independent space where public archival work can exist purely on its own merit. No algorithms. Just pure art from ghosts.
